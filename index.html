About Us

The NTU Staff Team is a friendly soccer club open to all NTU staff members. Whether you're a seasoned player or picking up the game for the first time, we welcome you to train, play, and connect with colleagues on the pitch. Every week, we play 11-a-side (main pitch, inside NTU) around 5:30pm on Fridays, and 5-a-side futsal (external pitch) around 7:00pm on Tuesdays.
